Diffraction Grating: Wavelength of a Laser

A laser is diffracted at a grating with 300 lines/mm. Measured: the distance x of the k-th maximum from the centre at screen distance L. From d·sin θ = k·λ with sin θ = x/√(x² + L²) the wavelength λ follows; it is compared with the literature value 632.8 nm (He-Ne laser).

How it works in PlotLab

  1. The columns “λ / nm” and “u(λ) / nm” are already calculated: λ = d·x/(k·√(x² + L²)) with d = 10⁶/300 nm, u(λ) from the error propagation with u(x) and u(L) (see the column heads).
  2. In the tool “Weighted mean” λ and u(λ) are chosen with k = 1. Press “Calculate”: weighted mean from the three orders.
  3. In the tool “Comparison” the mean (632.1 ± 0.9) nm and the literature value 632.8 nm (without uncertainty) are entered. Press “Compare”.

The numbers in the template are invented. You replace them with your own measurements.
