Volume Measurement with Error Propagation

First five readings of the diameter d with the vernier caliper (instrument error 0.05 mm), from them the cross-section area A with error propagation. Then three results for the volume V from three methods.

How it works in PlotLab

  1. In the tool “Mean and spread” the column “d / mm” and the instrument error 0.05 are entered. Press “Calculate”: mean with standard error and with instrument error (added in quadrature).
  2. The columns “A / mm²” and “u(A) / mm²” are already calculated: A = π·(d/2)², u(A) from Gaussian error propagation with u(d) = 0.05 mm (see the column heads).
  3. In the tool “Weighted mean” V and u are chosen with k = 1. Press “Calculate”: weighted mean with uncertainty and the overlap check.

The numbers in the template are invented. You replace them with your own measurements.
