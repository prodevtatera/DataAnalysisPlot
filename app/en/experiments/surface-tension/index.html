Evaluate Surface Tension by the Capillary Method

Capillary method with water: rise height h against the reciprocal of the tube radius r. From h = (2σ/(ρ·g))·(1/r) the surface tension σ follows from the slope.

How it works in PlotLab

  1. The column “r⁻¹ / mm⁻¹” (= 1/r) is a formula column and the x axis. Under “Results” press “Fit”: straight line h = a·(1/r) + b.
  2. In the tool “Derived quantity” σ = a·ρ·g/2000 (a in mm², σ in mN/m), ρ = 998 ± 1 kg/m³ and g = 9.81 m/s² are entered. Press “Calculate”.

The numbers in the template are invented. You replace them with your own measurements.
