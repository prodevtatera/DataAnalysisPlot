Newton's Rings: Radius of Curvature of a Lens

Diameter D of the dark rings k = 1 to 8 (sodium light). From r² = k·λ·R the radius of curvature R of the lens follows from the slope of r² against k.

How it works in PlotLab

  1. The columns “r² / mm²” = (D/2)² and “u(r²) / mm²” (error propagation from u(D)) are already calculated. Under “Results” press “Fit”: a = λ·R.
  2. In the tool “Derived quantity” R = a·1000/λ (a in mm², λ in nm, R in m) and λ = 589.3 ± 0.1 nm are entered. Press “Calculate”.

The numbers in the template are invented. You replace them with your own measurements.
