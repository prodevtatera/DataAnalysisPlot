String Pendulum: Determine the Acceleration g

String pendulum: period T at five string lengths l. Wanted: g from each row with mean, spread and 95 % confidence interval, and g from the slope of T² against l (T² = 4π²/g · l).

How it works in PlotLab

  1. The column “gi / m/s²” = 4π²·l/T² is already calculated. In the tool “Mean and spread” “gi / m/s²”, the divisor n−1 and the confidence interval 95 % are chosen. Press “Calculate”.
  2. The column “T² / s²” with “u(T²) / s²” (error propagation from u(T)) is the y axis. Under “Results” press “Fit”: a = 4π²/g in s²/m, weighted with 1/s².
  3. In the tool “Derived quantity” g = 4π²/a is entered. Press “Calculate”.

The numbers in the template are invented. You replace them with your own measurements.
