Mean, Spread and Histogram of a Measurement Series

30 readings of a length l. Wanted: mean, spread, frequency table, histogram with normal distribution and the reading at 16 % and 84 %.

How it works in PlotLab

  1. In the tool “Mean and spread” the column “l / mm” is chosen with the divisor n−1. Press “Calculate”: x̄ ± s/√n and s.
  2. In the tool “Histogram” the class width 0.2 mm and the start 9.5 mm are entered. Press “Calculate”: table and histogram with normal distribution. The tool “Cumulative plot” then shows the cumulative frequency with the reading at 16 % and 84 %.

The numbers in the template are invented. You replace them with your own measurements.
