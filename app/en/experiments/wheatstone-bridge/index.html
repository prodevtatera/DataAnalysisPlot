Wheatstone Bridge: Resistance with Uncertainty

Three balances of the bridge with different resistors R1, R2, R3 (all in Ω, with uncertainty). Wanted: the unknown resistance Rx = R1·R2/R3 with error propagation, the weighted mean and the comparison with the printed value (nominal value 220 Ω, tolerance 1 %).

How it works in PlotLab

  1. The columns “Rx / Ω” and “u(Rx) / Ω” are already calculated: Rx = R1·R2/R3, u(Rx) from Gaussian error propagation with u(R1), u(R2), u(R3) (see the column heads).
  2. In the tool “Weighted mean” Rx and u(Rx) are chosen with k = 1. Press “Calculate”: weighted mean and overlap check of the three balances.
  3. In the tool “Comparison” the weighted mean (219.4 ± 1.0) Ω and the nominal value (220 ± 2.2) Ω are entered. Press “Compare”: difference in combined uncertainties and verdict.

The numbers in the template are invented. You replace them with your own measurements.
