Refractometer: Calibration Line, Unknown Samples

Calibration line of a refractometer: refractive index n of sugar solutions of known concentration w. Wanted: the concentration of three unknown samples from their measured n, with uncertainty.

How it works in PlotLab

  1. Under “Results” press “Fit”: calibration line n = a·w + b, weighted with 1/s². The covariance of a and b is carried along for the next step.
  2. In the tool “Calibration line” three measured values n₀ are entered. Press “Read off x₀”: concentration w₀ = (n₀ − b)/a with uncertainty. The third sample lies outside the calibration series and gets a warning.

The numbers in the template are invented. You replace them with your own measurements.
