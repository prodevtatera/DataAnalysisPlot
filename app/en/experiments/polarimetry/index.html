Polarimetry: Specific Rotation from the Slope

Rotation angle α against the product of path length d and concentration c. The slope is the specific rotation [α].

How it works in PlotLab

  1. The column “d·c / dm·g·mL⁻¹” (= d·c) is a formula column and the x axis. Under “Results” press “Fit”: a is the specific rotation in °/(dm·g·mL⁻¹), weighted with 1/s².

The numbers in the template are invented. You replace them with your own measurements.
