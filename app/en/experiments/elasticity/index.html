Young's Modulus from Loading and Unloading a Wire

A steel wire is loaded and unloaded (extension ΔL against force F). Wanted: the slope of both series, their comparison and Young's modulus E from the loading.

How it works in PlotLab

  1. In the tool “Several series” loading and unloading are entered. Press “Fit series”: two straight lines and the comparison of the slopes.
  2. Under “Results” press “Fit”: straight line through the loading series, weighted with 1/s².
  3. In the tool “Derived quantity” E = 4·L/(π·d²·a), L = 1 ± 0.002 m and d = 0.5 ± 0.01 mm are entered. Press “Calculate”.

The numbers in the template are invented. You replace them with your own measurements.
