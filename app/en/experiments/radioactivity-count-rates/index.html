Count Rates: Poisson or Gaussian with χ² Test

40 counts of the pulses in intervals of equal length (background and source). Wanted: is the distribution of the counts compatible with Poisson or Gaussian (χ² test, 5 %)?

How it works in PlotLab

  1. In the tool “χ² test” the column “N / 1”, class width 3 and start 3 are entered. Press “Calculate”: observed and expected numbers, χ², degrees of freedom, p and verdict for both distributions. Classes with an expected number below 5 are marked with *.

The numbers in the template are invented. You replace them with your own measurements.
