Common questions

Is PlotLab free?

Yes. Free, no signup, no install.

Does my data stay on my computer?

Yes. Everything is calculated in your browser. Your measurements are not uploaded.

Can I paste from Excel?

Yes. PlotLab reads CSV files with decimal comma or point and semicolon or comma. Paste a table from Excel with Ctrl/Cmd+V.

Which fits are there?

Straight line (weighted and unweighted), exponential, polynomial up to degree 6, sine, Gaussian and free formula.

How is the result rounded?

The result reads (value ± u) unit: u is rounded to one significant digit, or two if that digit is 1 or 2, and the value to match.
