Methods and checks

PlotLab calculates in your browser with Python (SciPy). Every calculation is checked against independent reference values, not against the library itself.

  • Uncertainty: for weighted fits PlotLab gives both options, scaled by √(χ²/DoF) or unscaled (from the individual uncertainties). The result says which method applies.
  • Straight-line fit: against the NIST data set Norris (36 points) slope, intercept, their errors and the residual sum of squares agree to 9 or more digits.
  • Other fits: against NIST data sets (Misra1a, Misra1d, BoxBOD, Eckerle4, Lanczos3, Gauss1, ENSO, Pontius, Wampler 1, 4 and 5) the parameters agree to 6.5 to 9 digits. A start where the fit gets stuck shows a message instead of a wrong number.
  • Rounding: The result reads (value ± u) unit: u is rounded to one significant digit, or two if that digit is 1 or 2, and the value to match.
  • Examples: the numbers in the experiment templates are invented.
