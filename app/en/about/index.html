About PlotLab

PlotLab is a free tool for evaluating and plotting lab data in your browser. Free, no signup, no install.

I am Luis and I build PlotLab independently. It is not affiliated with any university.

PlotLab aims to make evaluating lab data faster than in Excel: paste your measurements, fit a line with error bars, read off the correctly rounded result.

Everything is calculated in your browser. Your measurements are not uploaded.

Do you have a question, found a bug or have an idea for an experiment? Write to me. I read every message.
